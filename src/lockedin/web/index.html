How a week actually goes

The loop you repeat while an idea is being worked out.

01You set the questionOne paragraph on what you are trying to establish.
02It goes and worksExperiments, figures, derivations — kept in the bubble, not in a scrollback.
03It presentsA short deck with the soft spots named, when it needs your judgement.
04You mark upIt re-derives, argues back, and the history keeps your objection.

No compromise on the model

lockedin is an interface, not a model wrapper. Bring the subscription you already pay for, or a local model.

  • Claude, Codex, Gemini, GPT — or Qwen on your own GPU.
  • Your key stays yours; your work stays on your disk.
  • Switching model never costs you the bubble, the marks or the history.

Connecting a project takes one line

The 🤖 button on any bubble hands you a single command to paste. The steps below are only for doing it by hand.

macOS or Linux

Python 3.11+ required. Installs only the Scientist client in your user PATH.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HamidrezaKmK/lockedin/main/install.sh | bash

Windows PowerShell

Python 3.11+ required. Installs the client in local app data.

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HamidrezaKmK/lockedin/main/install.ps1 | iex
  1. AuthorizeSign in to your lockedin account in the browser.lockedin-scientist login --server https://lockedin.codes
  2. Choose a workspaceList your workspaces and select the one used across projects.lockedin-scientist workspaces switch <workspace-id-or-name>
  3. Pick a bubbleList the approved bubbles in the selected workspace.lockedin-scientist bubbles
  4. Stay in syncCreate a project-local bubble workspace that keeps reports synchronized in the background.lockedin-scientist sync <bubble-slug>
  5. Install a native skillSet up the lockedin-scientist skill once for the agent you use.lockedin-scientist <codex|claude|agy> setup
